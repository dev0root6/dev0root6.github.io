Pair up

Both of you enter the same pairing code. Whoever goes first creates the pair; the second person joins it. Nobody else can join after that.

Already paired? Add another device

Paste the access token from Settings on your other device. A pair only holds two people, so this is how a second phone or a reinstall gets back in.
